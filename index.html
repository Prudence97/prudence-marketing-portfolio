Day one

Things you'll need

Three channels run most of the day-to-day work. Get these set up first.

The product

Earn4rmAds in a minute

Earn4rmAds is a rewarded advertising platform run by Adverion Nigeria Limited and powered by Bloocode Technology Limited. Businesses run campaigns that ask people to watch video ads, answer surveys and polls, install apps or complete registrations. People earn airtime, data or cash for each completed task, and advertisers see the results on their dashboard.

  1. Find

    Research businesses, industries and decision-makers.

  2. Start talking

    Outreach, discovery calls, meetings and demos.

  3. Develop

    Understand the need, share a proposal, agree next steps.

  4. Activate

    Support sign-up, campaign setup and launch.

  5. Keep

    Check progress, pursue renewals and top-ups.

Remember: a prospect in the pipeline is not an active advertiser yet. Each stage needs its own follow-up and its own record.
Your toolbox

The documents and how to use them

Each one follows the same pattern: when to use it, how to use it, and one thing to remember.

Essential

Advertiser pipeline / CRM

When
Every time you contact, meet or hear from a prospect.
How
Search before adding to avoid duplicates. Record the decision-maker, the stage, the last contact date and the next follow-up. Note responses and agreed actions.
The pipeline is only useful when it shows what is happening today.
Automation

CRM reminders

When
Each morning, and whenever a reminder email lands.
How
A Google Apps Script flags overdue prospects and emails a reminder. Open the record, check the dates and status, then act.
Check whether the prospect already replied before you follow up.
Platform

Advertiser dashboard

When
Checking an advertiser's account, campaigns or wallet.
How
Look at account status, campaign status, wallet balance, dates and performance. The Advertiser Dashboard Metrics Guide explains every number.
Never promise a launch, funding or a fix until the team confirms it.
Weekly

KPI and activity report

When
Every Friday.
How
Report four things separately: activity (contacted), pipeline movement (meetings, proposals), outcomes (approved accounts, live campaigns) and retention (renewals, top-ups).
Report what actually happened, what is outstanding and what is next.
Selling

Sales deck and proposals

When
Outreach, discovery meetings and demos.
How
Learn the client's goal first, then pick the selling points that fit. Strong sectors: fintech, FMCG, e-commerce, telecoms, agencies, health tech, edtech and media.
Use the current approved deck only.
Partners

Affiliate, aggregator and VAS materials

When
Talking with referral partners, agencies or VAS providers.
How
Know which document is for which partner: the affiliate programme and sign-up form, the terms and conditions, and the VAS offer or insertion order.
Some of these need management approval before you share them.

The people

Working with the team

Get to know the people you work with and how each person contributes. Management sets priorities, reviews progress and gives direction, so come to those conversations prepared and with clear updates.

Learn each person's role, the preferred channel for each kind of message and how approvals work. When you need help, explain the situation, say what you have already checked and name the decision or support you need.

Like any growing business, priorities shift. Staying organised, adapting and raising issues early will carry you a long way.

From experience

Eight things I learnt

  1. 1
    Keep the pipeline current.Update it right after every important call, email or meeting.
  2. 2
    Give every prospect a next step.Write when and why you will follow up. Avoid "contact later".
  3. 3
    Understand before you pitch.App installs and consumer research are different conversations.
  4. 4
    Make follow-ups useful.Refer to the last conversation and suggest a clear next step.
  5. 5
    Check before you commit.Confirm specs, pricing, approvals and funding with the team first.
  6. 6
    Track activity and outcomes separately.Emails sent and campaigns launched are different numbers.
  7. 7
    Start with the time-sensitive work.Promised callbacks and near-launch deals come before research.
  8. 8
    Improve the process as you learn it.Suggest a tracker or template, and confirm changes first.

    Case studies

    What I built and how it went

    1

    Enterprise and agency prospecting

    ResearchOutreachDiscovery

    Challenge. A new platform needed conversations with established brands that had never heard of it.

    What I did. I built a ranked prospect list of nearly 500 brands, organised it into outreach waves and reached decision-makers by email, phone, LinkedIn and WhatsApp. I followed up with discovery meetings and in-person visits.

    Result. Meetings with brands including UAC Foods, Promasidor, Deekay Group, Sanlam Allianz, Utiva, Tuteria and Total Health Trust, and conversations with Unilever Nigeria, Flour Mills and First Bank.

    2

    Pipeline and follow-up automation

    Google SheetsApps ScriptEmail automation

    Challenge. The team was tracking hundreds of prospects, follow-up dates and pending approvals by memory.

    What I did. I structured the pipeline into nine clear stages, from interest confirmed to pilot live, and wrote Google Apps Script reminders that email the team and flag overdue prospects.

    Result. A process that keeps running after I leave. It powers weekly pipeline reviews and KPI reports.

    3

    Pilot onboarding and reactivation

    OnboardingAccount management

    Challenge. Interested advertisers often stalled between signing up and going live.

    What I did. I walked advertisers through registration, explained campaign formats, chased creatives and checked what was blocking each launch. Once campaigns ended, I shared their results and discussed renewals.

    Result. 22 pilot advertisers created accounts and 17 brands and partners now run live campaigns, including Postly, Frames on Flames, BuyScrap Nigeria, Seemless HR and Crowdy Socials. In October I also restarted 9 low-running campaigns and brought back a dormant advertiser.

    4

    VAS, aggregator and affiliate partnerships

    PartnershipsCommercial terms

    Challenge. Direct sales alone grow slowly. Partners who already serve advertisers can bring them in faster.

    What I did. I introduced the platform to NitroSwitch, Glo Marketing, HML Consulting, VDL Technologies, Fundam Digitals and other VAS providers and aggregators, ran demo calls, and shared proposed test offers. I mapped 100 affiliate prospects and agreed referral and commission terms with management before offering them.

    Result. NitroSwitch is now a working partner with campaigns live on the platform, and partnership talks continue with the other providers.

    5

    Sales materials and reporting

    Pitch decksRate cardsKPI reports

    What I did. I produced pitch decks, pilot explainers, sign-up guides, sample insertion orders and campaign reports. I also wrote weekly KPI reports that track activity, pipeline movement, outcomes and retention separately.

    Result. Every conversation had a clear next step and the right document to support it.
